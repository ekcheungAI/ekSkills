## What Is Current

### Modular hierarchy

Bento and modular grids are mainstream for complex content because they make density scannable. Use mixed proportions, not equal cards. Good for feature sets, dashboards, product ecosystems, proof blocks, and content hubs.

Avoid when the page becomes a wall of rounded cards with no editorial rhythm.

### Evolved glass and spatial layering

Glassmorphism has matured into subtle translucency, depth, refraction, and elevation cues. Use it to separate layers, clarify hierarchy, or make controls feel material. Pair with restrained borders, soft shadows, and strong contrast.

Avoid heavy blur, unreadable backgrounds, random frosted panels, and decoration-only glass.

### Motion as interface language

Motion now explains state changes, cause and effect, loading, navigation, and story progression. Use 150-300ms UI transitions, scroll reveals with clear pacing, and reduced-motion fallbacks.

Avoid scroll hijacking, motion that blocks reading, and animation that exists only to impress.

### Type as design

Typography carries brand voice. Use expressive display faces, large editorial scale, variable fonts, tight hierarchy, and motion only when it improves attention. Pair expressive headings with highly readable body text.

Avoid generic giant headings, random all-caps, excessive font moods, and gradient text as a premium shortcut.

### Organic anti-grid and human warmth

Brands are pushing against sterile AI sameness with tactile texture, hand-made details, organic layouts, human illustration, nostalgic forms, and imperfect accents. Keep the core structure rational; let warmth appear in material, imagery, or type.

Avoid fake messiness, illegible layouts, and novelty that hurts navigation.

### Functional 3D and WebGL

3D is strongest for product inspection, configurators, spatial previews, data visualization, and memorable brand scenes. Use optimized assets, lazy loading, static fallback, and mobile performance checks.

Avoid heavy 3D that does not explain the product or blocks critical content.

### AI-aware interfaces

AI interfaces need transparency, editability, undo paths, clear boundaries, and user control. Use copilot surfaces, contextual suggestions, preview-before-apply flows, confidence/provenance cues, and human override.

Avoid autonomous actions that surprise users, fake intelligence, and unclear generated content.

### Prompt-specified motion heroes

AI site builders produce better heroes when the prompt behaves like a technical design spec: tokens, layout, typography, media, animation, component states, and implementation stack. MotionSites-style examples emphasize high-impact hero sections, animated backgrounds, liquid glass, micro-interactions, and React/Tailwind/Framer Motion-style specificity.

Avoid vague requests like "make a modern dark landing page." Specify the exact visual system and interaction behavior.

## Direction Picker

Pick one primary direction and one supporting accent:

- Premium SaaS: structured modular hierarchy, calm palette, subtle spatial depth, confident product visuals.
- Creative studio: expressive type, off-grid editorial rhythm, strong imagery, one memorable interactive motif.
- Ecommerce/product: product-first hero, tactile materials, comparison clarity, clear purchase path.
- Portfolio/editorial: type-led identity, image cadence, scroll storytelling, restrained navigation.
- AI product: transparent copilot interaction, trust cues, dark or neutral surfaces, precise microcopy.
- Motion-led launch page: high-impact hero, structured prompt spec, animated media, clear CTA, restrained follow-up sections.
- Local/cultural brand: human texture, place-specific imagery, bilingual typographic sensitivity, warm accent palette.

## Anti-Trend Discipline

Reject a trend when it:

- Slows the page without improving comprehension.
- Reduces contrast, readability, or keyboard/touch access.
- Makes the product look like an AI template.
- Adds a visual metaphor the copy and product cannot support.
- Repeats across every section.
- Forces the user to learn navigation before understanding the offer.
